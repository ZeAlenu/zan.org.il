# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, deployed as a Cloudflare Worker. The builders chose this stack before the current site work.

## Users

Primary audience: citizens of Israel.

Their main jobs, in order:

1. Decide whether to join the movement. Success is joining.
2. As members, find activity, updates, or a way to take part.

The site also serves professional staff in government and the private sector.

People looking for policy research and field work are an audience too, after the two jobs above.

## Product Purpose

zan.org.il is the public homepage of זה עלינו. Citizens of Israel use it to join and take part. The other audiences above use it to reach the organization.

The live page is a splash. Do not extend that page.

## Positioning

The public line, set in the final logo, is בונים תקומה ציונית.

This homepage is the organization's public home. The policy research and field work in the separate `ZeAlenu/platform` repository are not this product. The homepage may host that work, link to it, or leave it alone. That choice is still open.
